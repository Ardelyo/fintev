brand sheet · v1

Money that has a mood.

Fintev explains money in plain words, and shows it with soft shapes. A small character, Pip, reads the room — so a number never has to feel like a verdict.

01 · Language

Shapes → Blur → Design

Every screen starts as a handful of flat, friendly shapes. We blur them until they become light, then lay clear, quiet interface on top.

  • Round, never sharp. Cards sit at 34px, buttons are pills, even charts end in soft caps.
  • Colour is a mood, not a code. The backdrop shifts gently as your money does.
  • One serif voice. Fraunces speaks in italics when it’s being human.

02 · Character

Meet Pip

Pip is a drop of money that grew a face. Its expression is a handful of numbers — eye height, mouth curve, brow tilt — that tween smoothly between moods. Move your pointer: Pip watches.

    06 · Voice

    Say it like a friend

    Short, plain and warm. Never alarmist, never cute about debt.

    Say

    • “You’re worth $248,610.”
    • “Rent is already set aside.”

    Not

    • “Net worth: $248,610.00 USD”
    • “Liabilities scheduled.”
    • “Purchase denied.”
    • “WARNING: budget exceeded!”
